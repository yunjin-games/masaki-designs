Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

  1. We strive to properly protect information about individual customers ("personal information") under strict control.
  2. When we collect personal information, we inform you of the purpose of collection and of our point of contact, and collect only what is necessary.
  3. We use personal information only within the scope of its purpose, manage it appropriately, and do not disclose or provide it to third parties without your consent unless there are special circumstances.
  4. We keep personal information accurate and up to date, and take security measures to prevent unauthorized access, loss, destruction, alteration and leakage.
  5. When we outsource the processing of personal information, we contractually require the contractor not to leak or re-provide it, and we ensure proper supervision of the contractor.
  6. Personal information entrusted to us by a client is strictly managed and used only within the scope of the contract.
  7. We comply with the laws and standards that apply to the personal information we hold, and we continuously review and improve the measures described above.

Handling of Personal Information

We use personal information only to provide the services you request and within the proper scope of our business. Personal information received through requests for materials, inquiries and similar contacts will never be disclosed, provided or reused externally without your prior consent.
